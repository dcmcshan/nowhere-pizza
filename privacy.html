Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 August 2026.

The short version

This website does not run advertising trackers, and it does not set analytics cookies. We collect personal information only when you hand it to us — by placing an order or sending us a message.

What we collect

  • Online orders Your name, mobile number, optional email, your order, and any notes you add. This is passed to our point-of-sale system so the kitchen can make and hand over your food.
  • Messages Whatever you type into the contact form, plus the name and email you give us, so we can reply.
  • Your basket Stored in your own browser (local storage) so your order survives a page refresh. It never leaves your device until you place the order.

Who we share it with

Order details go to our point-of-sale provider so we can fulfil the order. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our ordering endpoint runs on Cloudflare. Both keep standard server logs, which may include your IP address, for security and reliability.

Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google when you load a page.

How long we keep it

Order records are retained in our point-of-sale system for as long as we need them for accounting and tax purposes. Messages are kept until they've been dealt with, and then a reasonable while after.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call either location and ask for a manager, or use the contact form. Clearing your browser's storage for this site removes your saved basket immediately.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: call Keystone on 970-485-6974 or Copper on 970-475-4373, or use the contact form.

Note for the operator: this is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. Have counsel confirm it covers your obligations — including CPA (Colorado Privacy Act) duties — and add your registered business name and a postal address for data requests before you rely on it.
