The Real Kind

The largest absinthe collection in Summit County, served the way it was meant to be.

Since the early 1800s

Elixir of intellect and vision

The Green Fairy was muse to the artist. Absinthe sat behind the work of Vincent van Gogh and Pablo Picasso, and the writing of Hemingway and Lewis Carroll — the elixir of intellect and vision.

Banned, mythologised, and eventually vindicated. What was blamed on the wormwood turned out to be the era's cheap adulterants and its sheer strength. The ritual survived all of it.

The ritual

  1. A measure of absinthe in the glass.
  2. Slotted spoon across the rim, sugar on the spoon.
  3. Iced water, drop by drop.
  4. The louche blooms. Wait for it.

Ask the bar to walk you through it. That's the point of keeping a collection this size.
