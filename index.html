The Green Fairy Is Our Muse

The real kind

Since the early 1800s the Green Fairy was muse to the artist — absinthe, the elixir of intellect and vision, behind the work of Van Gogh and Picasso and the writing of Hemingway and Carroll.

We keep the largest absinthe collection around, and we serve it the old way: slotted spoon, sugar, iced water, patience.
